# Street Walk review

The film opens at street level and shows how to drop the walker onto the map. The drag preview includes building footprints, public ground, walkable routes and the yellow landing marker. The end card holds the New York map address over the Brooklyn Bridge.

The driving montage runs from 14.5 to 22 seconds. It cuts from Times Square to One World Trade Center, the Vessel and Central Park. All four takes use the game's own simulation and map geometry. The camera for the Vessel tracks the car from the side so the car and the landmark are visible together. Two staged pixel pedestrians tumble in the Times Square take. The recorded contact times are 15.30 and 15.63 seconds in the film, with a short sound at each.

Reviewed the HyperFrames beat sheet, cuts and phone-sized sheet, then inspected decoded frames from each driving shot. HyperFrames reported zero lint, runtime, layout and contrast errors. The export has 900 frames at 1920 × 1080, H.264 video and stereo AAC. A full decode found no black gaps. Audio was verified by measurement at −17.1 LUFS integrated and −4.1 dBTP.
