# Street Walk announcement

30 seconds at 1920 × 1080 and 30 fps. Show where Street Walk lives and how to enter it, then give the viewer a reason to try New York from the ground.

The film uses actual footage of the New York map and the 3D viewer. Feature titles use Archivo bold at 69–120 px on flat enamel signs. The end card uses the site's Tiny5 wordmark, paper lettering and taxi yellow. Text starts at least 190 px from the sides. Motion comes from the map zoom, the draggable character and the game camera. Titles reveal over 7–10 frames and hold still.

| Time        | Picture and copy                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–2.5 s     | First-person Times Square hook. “Street Walk” and “New York” establish the feature.                                                                                                 |
| 2.5–8 s     | The map zooms in. “Drag the walker onto a street” describes the live gesture. The towers dim to reveal streets, building footprints and public spaces, with the yellow snap marker. |
| 8–14.5 s    | Walk and jump through street-level Times Square while traffic and animated billboards move.                                                                                         |

| 16.6–18.4 s | The car continues through Lower Manhattan with One World Trade Center in the skyline.                                                                                               |
| 18.4–20.2 s | A side tracking shot past the Vessel in Hudson Yards.                                                                                                                               |
| 20.2–22 s   | A wide view of the car driving alongside Central Park.                                                                                                                              |
| 22–26 s     | A first-person walk across the Brooklyn Bridge.                                                                                                                                     |
| 26–30 s     | The Tiny Worlds wordmark and “Open the New York map” close over the same moving shot.                                                                                               |

The soundtrack is an instrumental generated for this edit in Suno. Short map, reveal, pass-by and crossing sounds play under the music. Avoid mock controls, lens effects, decorative HUDs and exposition about features beyond the shots.
