# Pull back

```text
GOAL:    A stranger sees that it is the whole city, that they can look anything up in it, and
         that there are more cities: they go and open one.
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps
TIME:    31 s x 30 = 930 frames; music 120 BPM, 15 frames a beat, 60 a bar, first beat at 0.04 s
LOOK:    The site itself. Ground: the page's fog, #e9e6df. Signs: black enamel #15171a, paper
         lettering #f4f2ec, 20 px corners, the viewer's own shadow doubled; Archivo 600, 56 px
         for a line, 88 px for the title, -1% tracking. One accent, taxi yellow #f7c21b: the
         title's top line, the end card's address, and wherever the site already uses it.
         Medium: the real viewer filmed at twice its size (960 x 540 CSS px), its own tilt blur;
         the real front page captured at twice its size.
OBJECTS: the Empire State Building's crown and mast; Manhattan; the five boroughs, outlined and
         named by the viewer's district lines; the viewer's search
         sign, tool column, minimap and credit line; the Statue of Liberty on its star fort; a
         ferry and its pier; the front page's six city cards; its hero and sign.
MOTION:  camera: the take's own (scale in octaves, by the second, on a curve that never
         overshoots; cubic in-out elsewhere; the viewer's flyTo for the search). Signs: steps(3), 6 frames in, 5 out: pixels do not ease.
         Pointer: cubic in-out, 11-12 frames a move. Picture into card: power3 in-out, 15 frames.
SHOTS:   0.00-6.3    out from the Empire State Building's crown to the whole city, never cutting:
                     slow for half a second so the crown can be read, the whole tower by 2.1,
                     Manhattan by 5, at rest on the city by 6.3. Reads "Every building at its
                     real height" (2.04-4.5).
         6.54-8.04   "All five boroughs": the viewer's own district lines draw Manhattan,
                     Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island, one every half beat, each lit
                     while it is the newest, then all five stand outlined and named. A soft tick each.
         8.04-10.3   the title sign lands on the bar the music opens: "Whole cities, pocket-sized".
                     Hands off: the outlines go (9.74) and the sign shrinks onto the place the
                     viewer's search sign appears.
         10.04-14.3  the viewer's signs; the pointer clicks the field (10.54), "statue of liberty"
                     is typed, the first result is clicked (12.54): the map flies there. Click,
                     keys, click, whoosh.
         14.3-17.2   landed at the statue's foot; the view backs off until the whole figure shows.
         17.2-20.04  on to the slip: a ferry comes in. Reads "Free to explore" (18.34-19.96).
         20.04-22.04 the signs go; out to the whole city. Riser.
         22.04-25.04 PAYOFF: the picture shrinks onto New York's card on the front page (lands
                     22.54, impact); six cities, each alive.
         25.04-26.04 up the page to its hero.
         26.04-31.0  END CARD: the page's own hero and sign (wordmark, tagline, pitch, button);
                     at 28.05, the music's peak, "Want yours next?" and the address. Pop.
BANNED:  a dark void; glow, particles; gradients; fades and slide-ups on signs (they step);
         a mockup of anything the site has; a cut inside the take; a docked logo; the fan-made
         places (they stay out of what promotes the project); the address in this folder.
QA:      0.1 the crown, sharp, nothing blank. 2.3 the whole tower. 7.2 a borough lit, the
         earlier ones outlined. 8.3 title over the whole city, five boroughs named. 10.3 the real search
         sign where the title went. 12.5 the pointer on the first result. 16.5 the whole statue.
         19 the ferry, the line clear of the viewer's signs. 22.4 the picture over the cards.
         23.5 six cards. 29.5 the hero, both signs, the address.
```

## The two films not made

- **Find your corner**: the search as the whole film, three places in three cities and three
  languages, each arrival handing off to the next search.
- **Six cities, one beat**: the wordmark filling the frame, the city showing through its
  letters, the last three letters, the colour and the map changing on every beat.
